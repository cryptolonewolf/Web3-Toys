# Who Built It? portraits

The game works without pictures: until a portrait is added, each person
shows a pencil-style sketch with their initials. Add portraits whenever they
are ready, a few at a time.

## Style

Match the Aki mascot (`assets/mascot/aki.webp`): a hand-drawn coloured-pencil
sketch, loose scribbled hatching, dark outlines, warm skin tones, bright pink,
blue and yellow accents, head and shoulders, friendly expression, plain white
or transparent background.

Prompt to start from (attach `aki.webp` as the style reference):

> Hand-drawn coloured-pencil portrait sketch of [NAME], head and shoulders,
> friendly expression, loose scribbled hatching, bold dark outlines, warm
> skin tones, bright pink, blue and yellow accents, plain white background,
> square. Same drawing style as the reference image.

Notes:

- Draw from a photo you are allowed to use (their own press photo, or one
  with a free licence on Wikimedia Commons). Keep it a fair, friendly
  likeness: no mocking caricatures, including for the cautionary tales.
- Some AI image tools refuse to draw real people. An illustrator can do the
  50 in one batch with the same brief.
- Satoshi Nakamoto, Meow and ZachXBT have never shown their faces: draw a
  hooded figure with a question mark, or leave them; the game already shows
  a hooded sketch for them.
