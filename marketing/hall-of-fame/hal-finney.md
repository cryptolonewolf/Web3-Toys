## Story thread (@web3toys)

1/ Hal Finney, in three sentences 🧵

2/ Hal was a cryptographer who worked on PGP, the email encryption program.
3/ In 2004 he built reusable proof of work, an early digital token.
4/ He was one of the first people to run Bitcoin and got its first transaction from Satoshi in 2009.
5/ He later lived with ALS and passed away in 2014.

6/ Known for: The first Bitcoin transaction (2009), Reusable proof of work (2004), PGP encryption. Meet all 50 Web3 Hall of Famers: https://web3.toys/hall-of-fame/hal-finney/
